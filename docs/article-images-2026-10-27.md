## Final generation prompts

The six assets used the built-in image generator in the `photorealistic-natural` mode. Hero prompts requested safe, documentary-style removal preparation in Dubai villas or apartments, with plain dark-green workwear and protected work areas. In-body prompts requested orderly assessment workshops showing the actual shell, motor, control, filter, duct, blade and hardware groups relevant to each article. Every prompt excluded text, logos, watermarks, unsafe electrical handling, leaks, sparks, curbside dumping and generic roll-off dumpsters.
