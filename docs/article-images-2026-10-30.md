## Final generation prompts

The six assets used the built-in image generator in `photorealistic-natural` mode. Hero prompts requested calm documentary scenes in modern Dubai apartments: a one-item service-lift pickup, upright refrigerator movement, and a grouped handover load. In-body prompts requested an access-ready photo inventory, fridge emptying and water-line preparation, and separated recovery streams at a tower loading area. Every prompt excluded logos, readable text, watermarks, unsafe lifting, uncontrolled waste, blocked fire routes and generic roll-off dumpsters.
