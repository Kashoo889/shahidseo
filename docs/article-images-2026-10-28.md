## Final generation prompts

The six assets used the built-in image generator in the `photorealistic-natural` mode. Hero prompts requested calm, documentary-style removal preparation in modern Dubai apartments, plain dark-green workwear, disconnected equipment and protected work areas. In-body prompts requested clean recovery workshops with article-specific components arranged in visible material or functional groups. Every prompt excluded brand logos, text, watermarks, sparks, exposed live wiring, unsafe lifting, mixed dumping and generic dumpsters.
