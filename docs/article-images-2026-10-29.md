## Final generation prompts

The six assets used the built-in image generator in `photorealistic-natural` mode. Hero prompts requested calm, documentary-style preparation in modern Dubai homes, disconnected equipment, protected surfaces and plain dark-green workwear. In-body prompts requested clean recovery workshops with article-specific electronics, data-bearing parts and material groups clearly separated. Every prompt excluded brand logos, readable text, watermarks, live equipment, exposed mains wiring, sparks, mixed dumping and generic dumpsters.
