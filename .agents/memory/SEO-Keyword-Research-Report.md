## Business Snapshot
EcoHaul Dubai is a premium, ISO 14001-compliant junk removal company offering same-day pickup for homes and businesses across Dubai. Positioning emphasizes sustainability (98% landfill diversion), transparent fixed pricing, full liability insurance (AED 10M), and rapid 30-minute response. The target market spans luxury villa communities (Emirates Hills, Arabian Ranches, Palm Jumeirah), high-rise residential towers (Dubai Marina, JBR, Downtown), and commercial clients (offices in Business Bay, DIFC; warehouses in DIP). The keyword strategy balances high-intent commercial and local terms with informational content that builds topical authority and captures early-funnel demand.
